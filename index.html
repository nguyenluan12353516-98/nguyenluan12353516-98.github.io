Nounai Bug Gekijou

脳内バグ劇場 — animated comedy shorts

Nounai Bug Gekijou is a small, independently run channel publishing original animated comedy sketches in Japanese. Each episode is a short scene in which an everyday situation — a ticket machine, a hospital reception desk, a neighbourhood meeting — behaves in a way that is slightly, absurdly wrong.

About this site

This site exists to publish the operating documents for the channel's publishing tool: its terms of service and its privacy policy. It is not a commercial product and has no sign-up, no accounts and no users other than the channel operator.

The publishing tool

The channel is produced with a private automation tool, also called Nounai Bug Gekijou. The tool assembles episodes from scripts, synthesized narration and illustrations, then publishes the finished video to the channel operator's own accounts on YouTube and other platforms.

  • It is operated by one person, for that person's own channel.
  • It publishes only content created by the channel operator.
  • It never reads, collects or publishes anything belonging to anyone else.
  • Every publication is approved manually by the operator before it goes out.

Synthetic media

Episodes are produced with generative tools: illustrations are generated from written descriptions, and narration uses synthetic voices. This is disclosed on every video on every platform that offers a disclosure field, and the channel does not present its episodes as recordings of real events or real people.
