Privacy Policy

Nounai Bug Gekijou (脳内バグ劇場) · effective 11 September 2026

Summary. This is a private publishing tool used by one person to post their own videos to their own social media accounts. It has no users other than its operator. It collects no personal information from anyone, and it shares nothing with anyone.

1. Who this covers

This policy covers the Nounai Bug Gekijou publishing tool (“the Tool”), a private program run on the channel operator's own computer. The Tool has no public interface, no registration, and no user accounts. The only person who uses it is the channel operator, referred to here as “the Operator”.

2. Information the Tool holds

The Tool stores the following, and only the following, on the Operator's own computer:

  • Access tokens issued by YouTube, TikTok and Meta after the Operator personally authorizes the Tool on each platform. These allow the Tool to upload videos to the Operator's own accounts.
  • Video files, scripts, images and captions created by the Operator for publication on the Operator's own channels.
  • Public performance statistics for the Operator's own videos — view counts, watch time, click-through rate — retrieved from the platforms' own analytics interfaces, used to decide what to make next.

3. Information the Tool does not collect

The Tool does not collect, request, store or process:

  • personal information about viewers, followers or any other person;
  • contact lists, messages, email, or any private communication;
  • location data, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers;
  • information belonging to any account other than the Operator's own;
  • payment information of any kind.

The Tool does not use cookies, analytics scripts or trackers. This website serves static pages only and sets nothing in your browser.

4. Where information is kept

Everything listed in section 2 is stored locally on the Operator's own computer. Access tokens are kept in a restricted folder outside the project's source code. Nothing is uploaded to any third-party server except the video content the Operator chooses to publish, which goes only to the Operator's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

5. Sharing and selling

Nothing is sold, rented, traded, or shared. There are no advertising partners, no data brokers, no analytics vendors and no third-party processors. The only outbound transfers are the video uploads described above, made to the platform the Operator is publishing to.

6. Retention and deletion

Access tokens are kept for as long as the Tool is in use and are deleted when the Operator revokes the Tool's access on the platform in question, or deletes the token file. Video production files are kept on the Operator's computer and may be deleted at any time. Because no other person's data is collected, there is no third-party data to retain or delete.

7. Your rights

If you are a viewer of the channel: the Tool holds no information about you, so there is nothing for you to access, correct or erase. Any information the platforms themselves hold about your use of them is governed by those platforms' own privacy policies, not this one.

8. Children

The Tool is not directed at children and is not used by anyone other than the Operator. It knowingly collects no information from anyone, including children.

9. Synthetic media

Videos published with the Tool contain computer-generated illustrations and synthetic narration. They depict fictional characters and invented situations. They do not depict real people, and no real person's likeness or voice is reproduced. This is disclosed on each video wherever the publishing platform provides a disclosure field.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Because the Tool has no users to notify, no other notice is given.
